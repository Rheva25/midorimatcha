THE MIDORI EXPERIENCE

A harmonious marriage of architectural calm, meditative chasen whisking, and honest botanical flavor.

01 / Architecture

Café Interior

Minimalist travertine stone, natural Hinoki woodwork, and tranquil courtyard pockets built to slow the pulse of modern life.

02 / Ceremony

Chasen Bar & Sifting

Every chawan is prepared fresh to order with authentic 100-prong bamboo whisks, calibrated water temperature, and single-cultivar cultivars.

03 / Sanctuary

Verandas & Tatami

Quiet pine wood nooks, sunken tatami seating, and open colonial verandas enveloped by heritage calm and tropical gardens.

04 / Libations

Signature Cold Foams

Vibrant layered iced ceremonial matcha lattes, seasonal fruit purees, and silky oat cloud creams made exclusively in-house.
