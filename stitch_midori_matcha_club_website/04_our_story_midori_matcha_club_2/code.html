Origin & Heritage

A Journey from the Hills of Uji

Our story began with a direct-trade kinship alongside 6th-generation tea farming families in the misty valleys of Wazuka and Uji, Kyoto. A terroir endowed with morning river fogs and mineral-rich soils that have nurtured the world's most revered tencha gardens since the Kamakura era.

1

100% Ceremonial First Harvest

Exclusively hand-picked first-flush tender spring leaves (ichibancha), dense with natural chlorophyll and delicate savory L-theanine umami.

2

Stone-Ground Precision

Slowly milled on artisanal granite stones at strictly regulated low speeds to preserve vital nutrients and prevent friction heat, yielding a silky 5–10 micron micronization.

3

Pure Botanical Integrity

Zero preservatives, artificial sweeteners, synthetic food dyes, or filler starches. 100% single-origin Japanese tencha botanicals.

The Discipline

The Fourfold Art of the Midori Whisk

From terroirs to your handcrafted Chawan, every movement is guided by meditative precision and uncompromising craft.

Chasen Aeration

Manual fine silver mesh sifting prior to aeration to ensure a cloud-like suspension with zero clumping.

The Shade & Harvest

Traditional 25-day canopy shading (Honzu) prior to spring harvest, filtering 90% of sunlight to enrich L-Theanine amino synthesis and create that signature luminous electric jade hue.

25-Day Shading
Step 02 hourglass_empty

Granite Stone Milling

Slow-revolving granite millstones turn rhythmically for 60 full minutes to yield just 35 grams of powder, preventing friction burn and preserving raw enzymatic purity.

35g / Hour Stone Yield
Step 03 waves

The Chasen Aeration

Vigorous rhythmic 'W' aeration with a 100-prong bamboo chasen in 75°C–80°C mineral spring water, lifting a velvety micro-foam crema with toasted nutty fragrance.

Precise 78°C Extraction

Modern Pairing
