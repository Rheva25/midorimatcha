## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a modern Japanese-inspired aesthetic tailored for an elevated matcha and dessert lifestyle house. Balancing tranquil heritage with high-fashion café editorial styling, the interface pairs expansive whitespace and asymmetric grid disciplines with expressive typographic pairings.

The audience encompasses urban design enthusiasts, wellness purists, and contemporary culinary seekers who appreciate craftsmanship, slow rituals, and deliberate modernity. 

Visual design is guided by:
- **Contemporary Minimalist Discipline**: Purposeful negative space, structural framing, and uncluttered composition.
- **Editorial Graphic Composition**: High-impact typographic scale contrasts with meticulous, quiet functional components.
- **Tactile Softness**: Organic rounded silhouettes, pill-shaped accents, and physical paper-like warmth that prevents the interface from feeling sterile.

## Colors

The color palette translates the natural pigments of ceremonial matcha, raw bamboo, and whipped dairy into a cohesive, high-contrast spectrum.

- **Primary (`#6F8F52` — Matcha Green)**: Conveys vibrancy, natural freshness, and premium botanical energy. Used for focal CTAs, selective brand badges, and active state indicators.
- **Secondary (`#243B2A` — Deep Forest)**: Grounded, high-authority tone evoking shaded tea canopies. Used for primary typography, structural architectural lines, and inverted editorial blocks.
- **Tertiary (`#F3E7A5` — Butter Yellow)**: A delicate pastry and dairy accent that injects warmth and energetic counterpoint into the green spectrum. Used for celebratory moments, badges, and secondary callouts.
- **Neutral (`#F8F6EE` — Warm Cream)**: Replaces stark white with the soft tactile presence of unbleached washi paper. Acts as the foundational canvas tone across the default light mode.
- **Charcoal (`#242722`)**: Employed exclusively for micro-copy, technical specifications, and subtle dividers requiring absolute legibility without the harshness of pitch black.

## Typography

Typography functions as both voice and visual architecture. The coupling of **Epilogue** and **Plus Jakarta Sans** establishes an editorial dynamic:

- **Display & Headlines (Epilogue)**: Imparts structural geometric weight, characterful terminals, and an avant-garde editorial presence. Oversized tracking is kept tight (`-0.03em` to `-0.04em`) to maintain compositional density on editorial titles.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Delivers clear, rounded, and contemporary readability that softens technical metadata and ordering details.
- **Label Caps (Epilogue)**: Reserved for taxonomic labels, harvest dates, ceremonial grades, and navigation sub-headers to establish rhythm through deliberate tracked-out uppercase styling.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy merges an asymmetrical 12-column fluid grid on desktop with large visual breathing room to evoke an airy gallery experience.

- **Desktop (1024px and up)**: 12-column grid with dynamic multi-column spans (e.g., 7:5 ratio for hero product shots against narrative copy). Outer margins scale generously (`margin-desktop`) to center and focus consumption.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column layout. Asymmetrical offsets collapse into balanced 4:4 or full-width stacked blocks while retaining ample horizontal guttering.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4-column layout prioritizing vertical scroll rhythms. Bleed-edge imagery shifts into rounded offset banners (`margin: 1.25rem`).

Vertical pacing enforces generous cadence: section blocks leverage rhythmic increments of `space-xl` and larger to sustain an unhurried, luxury tempo.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh, generic drop shadows in favor of subtle surface-container layering and natural ambient light:

- **Tonal Layering**: Depth is primarily established through surfaces stacked atop `#F8F6EE` (Warm Cream). Elevated panels and modals adopt crisp pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or tinted Butter Yellow (`#F3E7A5`) surfaces.
- **Ambient Green Cast Shadows**: When elevation is required for hovering cards or floating cart trays, the shadow uses a diffused, organic formulation:
  - Blur radius: 24px to 40px
  - Spread: -4px
  - Color: `#243B2A` with 6% to 10% opacity, providing a grounded vegetal shadow rather than an artificial gray cast.
- **Low-Contrast Framing**: Structural cards and editorial containers use a 1px border colored with `#243B2A` at 10% opacity, providing crisp edge definition without breaking minimalist flow.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a high-curvature shape language (`roundedness: 3`) to echo the hand-carved curvature of chawan matcha bowls, bamboo whisks, and soft mochi pastries:

- **Pill Geometry (`rounded-full`)**: Applied to all primary interactive elements including buttons, floating chips, category toggles, and metadata status badges.
- **Generous Containers (`rounded-xl` / 3rem)**: Utilized on editorial image masks, hero card frames, and product showcase enclosures.
- **Asymmetric Image Masking**: Curated photography containers may mix corner radii (e.g., combining 3rem on diagonally opposed corners with 1rem on adjacent corners) to produce custom architectural window frames.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Full pill shape, filled with `#243B2A` (Deep Forest) text in `#F8F6EE` (Warm Cream). On hover, transitions smoothly to `#6F8F52` (Matcha Green). Padding: `0.875rem` vertical by `2rem` horizontal.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Full pill shape, transparent background, 1.5px solid `#243B2A`. On hover, fills with `#243B2A` with inverted cream text.
- **Accent**: Filled with `#F3E7A5` (Butter Yellow), text in `#243B2A`. Used for member perks, checkout triggers, and promotional alerts.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Fully rounded pills displaying taxonomic metadata (e.g., "Ceremonial Grade", "Uji Harvest", "Cold Whisked").
- **Inactive**: Warm cream background with a 1px border in `#243B2A` (15% opacity), text in `#242722`.
- **Active**: Filled with `#6F8F52`, text in `#FFFFFF`, without a visible border.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- Constructed with smooth 2rem (`rounded-lg`) or 3rem (`rounded-xl`) outer radiuses on a pure white or butter-tinted surface.
- Imagery dominates the upper 70% of the card, framed in an inner rounded canvas with an ambient tint.
- Meta-information uses `label-caps` in deep forest alongside price points set in `title-md`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Enclosed pill-shaped or 1rem-rounded fields resting on `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border in `#243B2A` (15% opacity).
- Focus states shift the border to 1.5px `#6F8F52` accompanied by a 3px soft focus ring tinted at 15% opacity.
- Labels sit outside the field using `label-md` uppercase styling for immediate clarity.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Radio Buttons**: Dual concentric rings; outer ring in `#243B2A` with an inner filled circle in `#6F8F52` when active.
- **Checkboxes**: 0.375rem softened corners, filling with `#243B2A` upon selection and displaying a warm cream check glyph.

### Specialized Component: Flavor & Cultivar Matrix
- A sensory indicator for single-origin matcha profiles (e.g., Umami, Bitterness, Sweetness, Foam Density).
- Built using segmented horizontal pill bars filled dynamically with `#6F8F52` over a muted cream track.
